# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our Standards

Positive behaviors include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassment or discriminatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers directly.
